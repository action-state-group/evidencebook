- The subject convention v0 (evidencebook#116, #1013), byte for byte with Go: `Book.seal_subject_root` and `Book.close_subject` seal `subject_root` and `subject_close` records, and the book refuses what Go refuses (a second root for one id, any second root in a per-subject `log` book, a `log` root not at seq 1, a close that is not of exactly one root of the book or not the count the book computes, a second close, a record citing a field root without its id at the pointer, a member after its close, a member of two subjects). Statements are decoded as Go's struct decoding decodes them (case-insensitive member names, missing and `null` members as zero values, unknown members refused). The default authenticated indexes gain `subject_id`, whose root a checkpoint commits only once it holds a leaf, so a book with no subject writes what it did. Window and `book_close` records are left out of the indexes and a book ending in `book_close` commits no further index root, as in Go. The correlation reader takes a cited root's `subject_id` first. `evidencebook.subject.subjects_of_record` reads a record's subjects from a verified bundle's headers, and `evidencebook.subject_bundle.verify_subject_bundle` verifies `subject_bundle/0` in both forms over agent-action-capsule's Evidence Bundle verifier, checked against `testdata/subject`. Subject bundles are verified, not built.

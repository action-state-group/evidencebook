### Added: `Cadence.HistoryCard2`, the shelf's `history_card/2` staple (#626)

- `Cadence.HistoryCard2(ctx, n, window)` builds the profile's `history_card/2` over the cadence log as a staple: the finished ticks at the sampled positions `P(n-1, window)`, as of tick `n` (0 for the newest finished tick), each with the consistency proof from the one listed before it. It holds no record content and no deal counts, and `CheckStaple` accepts it for the shelf's signer. Only the sampled ticks are read, so it has no tick cap: the cadence's `history_card/1` staple is refused past `DefaultMaxAnswerItems` ticks (#354). Window 0 is `DefaultHistoryWindow`, and a window outside 1 to 64 is `ErrInvalid`. Offer staples carry `history_card/2` only (Steven's ruling u539). `Cadence.HistoryCard`, and `Book.Staple`'s `history_card/1` and `standard_history/1`, stay for callers that have not moved yet and are deprecated. Cards answered on a signed ask are unchanged.

### Changed: capsulectl-deal reads its own deal formats on capsule-cli v0.1.0-rc50 (#735)

- `capsulectl/go.mod` requires capsule-cli v0.1.0-rc50, whose core no longer checks deal formats (capsule-cli#609). capsulectl-deal now holds those checks itself, copied from capsule-cli v0.1.0-rc47 and credited: the deal report (x-deal-v0 and x-deal-v1, with the x-deal-v1 report rule), a stripped copy, the cadence chain (x-cadence-witness/v0) and its receipts, a sale's threads (x-deal-sale/v0) and a History selection (x-history-selection/v0). It reads them through the core's extension reader seam (`cli.ExtensionReader`), so the core's own result is never weakened.
- **`capsulectl deal verify --bundle FILE [--witness-directory FILE]`** is the reader verb: the core's `verify --bundle` with the deal reader. `capsulectl verify --bundle` in the core alone now checks only the neutral parts of a deal copy, and leaves its deal section uninterpreted. The plugin's page gates, its nested verdicts (a sale's threads, a History's deals, a composed page's members) and its share record go through the reader too. The core's own page verbs (`presentation list`, `presentation build`, `bundle --html`) pass no reader, so they judge only a deal copy's neutral parts: a History whose nested deal fails is INVALID through `capsulectl deal verify`, but `presentation build` writes its page.
- **One difference from rc47, by the seam's design:** a copy with a valid cadence chain beside a failing direct witness receipt now reads INVALID. rc47 let the cadence claim replace the direct one and read VALID; the core now keeps its own failing claim beside the reader's (`reader_witnesses`). Every other copy of the rc47 goldens (#738) reads as rc47 read it.
- The x-deal-v1 report rule is the plugin's own, no longer registered with the core (`SetDealV1ReportVerifier` is gone from capsule-cli).

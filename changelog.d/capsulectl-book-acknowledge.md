### Added: `capsulectl-book book acknowledge` and `book rebut` answer the counterparty's Close (#116)

- `book acknowledge` records that this book has seen the counterparty's Close for a period and agrees (an `acknowledgement` record, `acknowledges` link), which makes it AGREED; `book rebut --reason TEXT` records that it disputes it (a `rebuttal` record, `rebuts` link, statement `{"reason": TEXT}`), which makes it CONTESTED. The Close is the one `book close` would cite from the `--peer` bundle under the pinned key; its head need not be disclosed. The record goes in the current book (the current window on a windowed store) under a checkpoint. A repeat returns the first record from any window; acknowledging after a rebuttal of the same Close, and a second rebuttal with another reason, are refused. Neither checks the exchanges or holds the bundle: an acknowledgement is the person's statement, and the skill runs either only on their word.
- `book close --peer` against a counterparty's Close now holds the peer bundle it relies on (#1009) and prints it as `held_bundle`, on a repeat too; on a windowed store it seals in a window's grace again. `capsulectl/go.mod` requires the evidencebook commit with the grace fix.

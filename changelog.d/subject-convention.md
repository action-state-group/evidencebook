### Added: the subject convention v0, `subject_root`, `subject_close` and the subject bundle (#116)

- `Book.SealSubjectRoot` seals a `subject_root` record (`SubjectRootStatement`: `subject_kind`, `subject_id`, `membership`) declaring a subject by its shared exchange id. Its members are the records that cite it and carry the id at the `field:<pointer>` it names (`MembershipExchangeID` is `correlation.exchange_id`), or, for `log` membership, every record of a per-subject book after it; the root, a close and the holder's bookkeeping records are never members.
- `Book.CloseSubject` seals a `subject_close` record that `closes` the root and states the member count and the last member (`SubjectCloseStatement`). A subject need not close.
- The book refuses a second root for one id, a `log` root that is not the book's first record, a record citing a root without its id, a record in two subjects, a `subject_close` whose count is not the book's, a second close, and a member sealed after the close.
- `NewSubjectIDIndex`, the `subject_id` authenticated index, is a default index. It keys a subject's root, members and close by its id; a checkpoint commits its root only once it holds a leaf, so a book with no subject writes what it wrote before. `ProveKeyRange` over it commits even an empty root.
- A correlation subject reads a record's cited `subject_root` first, then `correlation.exchange_id`, then `subject_ref`.
- `Book.SubjectBundle` builds the `subject_bundle/0` derivation that a `SubjectBundleDefinition` registered with `RegisterDerivation` grants, or the holder's own copy. The full form is an Evidence Bundle v2 with an `evidencebook/subject` extension (`SubjectExtension`) at the `holder_commitment` level (the index's key range proof) or the `log_prefix` level (every record from seq 1 disclosed: a per-subject book, or `DiscloseLog`), listing the members' foreign citations by digest. The projection (`SubjectProjection`) carries the root and the visible members as their declared header fields, with no position, proof or close, so a protected member changes none of its bytes; its completeness is the holder's assertion. `VerifySubjectBundle` checks either form (`VerifiedSubject`). `Respond` does not serve a subject bundle definition. Vectors: `testdata/subject/`.

### Changed: a record named `subject_root` or `subject_close` is checked (#116)

- `Append` and `AppendCapsule` now read a record of type `subject_root` or `subject_close` as the subject convention defines it and refuse one that breaks it (`ErrInvalid`); before, `record_type` was open vocabulary for these names too. A record such a caller sealed earlier, whose statement is not a subject root, still opens and declares no subject.

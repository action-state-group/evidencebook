### Changed: x-deal-v1 states what agreeing to an adjudicator commits a party to (#898)

- **PROFILE.md.** An agreement that names an adjudicator agrees to answer its `deal_adjudicator_copy/1` request in full. A party that refuses or does not answer leaves the case to proceed without its book, and a ruling may go against it on records it signed itself. This is a statement of the existing rule; no record, check or code changes.

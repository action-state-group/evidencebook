### Changed: x-deal-v1 counts a deal whose counterparty agreed and then went silent (#511)

- **Start rule, design §5.2 step 8.** After `agree_by`, with both agreements joined, an offerer's agreement that has no record time and that the acceptor's book never names is now `started`, `local: true`, `by: null`, where it was `not_started` (Steven, u608: "count it"). The silent side's failure then shows in the status and on the card. A later witnessed time after `agree_by` still gives `not_started`, a naming gives `started` for good, and a timed late agreement that is not named stays `not_started`.
- **Go, Rust and Python** change together: `xdealv1.Start`, `deal::v1::start` and `evidencebook.deal.v1.start`, with the shared `testdata/deal-v1/start` and `report` vectors (a new `agree-then-silent` report case pins that its rows are listed).

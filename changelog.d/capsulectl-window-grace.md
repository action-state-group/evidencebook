- A windowed book store keeps a grace for a Close over an earlier window (evidencebook#807): `book window init --grace` fixes it with the window (whole days such as `7d`, or a duration such as `36h`, shorter than the period; default 7 days for month, quarter and year windows, 1 day for week, none for day), and `store init` and `book window init` print it (`window: month (calendar months, UTC), closes 7 days after it ends, fixed for this store`). `close` and `reconcile` over a period inside a window in its grace work in that window's book, and `close` names the window it sealed in; its `--bundle-out` there is anchored at that window's checkpoint and records no disclosure, since a window in grace takes only Closes, so the book does not show that bundle was handed over. `book window list` shows each window's `closes_at` and `in_grace`; `book window roll` and `publish` queue and deliver each window's boundary checkpoint to the witness with its final one; `book window chain` over a window in grace says when it closes.

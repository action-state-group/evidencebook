- `dealprofile`: a sale's own log takes a `sale_unpost` record, a listing taken down (PROFILE.md section 6, rule 15). Its body is `platform` only, it needs no check, evaluation or approval, and its one `unposts` ref names the live post act of that platform (the latest post on it, not already taken down); a later post makes the platform live again, and a taken-down listing no longer counts in `listing_classes`. The record type is new, so a verifier that predates it refuses a sale that holds one; a sale without one verifies as before (no `rule_inputs_version` change). New fixtures: `positive-sale-unpost/` and the `neg-sale-unpost-*` negatives (#451).

### Added: hand a standard_history card's material, and recompute its counts (#211)

- `Book.StandardHistoryBundle` (and `Cadence.StandardHistoryBundles`, one per committed log): the `evidence-bundle/v2` of a book's records from seq 1 to a checkpoint it issued, as `spec/standard-history-v0.md` §6 "Handing the material" (SH-3) carries it, with no new member. `StandardHistoryBundleRequest.Disclose` is the host's share gate: a record it refuses still travels as a digest-only capsule, so coverage can be shown without the record.
- `CheckStandardHistoryMaterial`: reports the card's six properties and recomputes the counts over the card's own window from the handed bundles. `counts_recompute` is `PASS` or `FAIL` only when coverage is shown for the anchor's log and for every other log the anchor commits (interval coverage and per-record membership established from seq 1 to the committed extent, anchored at the committed checkpoint), and the host's `DealMapping` read every member it needs. A partial handover, a withheld member (`ErrMaterialWithheld`) or an anchor that commits its logs through a salted or sparse tree (SH-10, which a cadence tick's anchor does) keeps it `NOT_CHECKED`, never `FAIL`.

### Added: an x-deal-v1 buyer's intent names the seller by commitment (#1031)

- A buyer's x-deal-v1 `intent` may carry `seller`: the seller's record key as the citing agreement's `counterparty_key` names it (`null` with no layer), and `sha256-jcs-nonce256` commitments of its name and, when given, its domain. The name and domain never appear in the clear; the buyer keeps them and their nonces. `xdealv1.IntentBody` (v0's body plus `Seller`) now decodes every intent, read beside v0's closed `body_intent`, and the schema holds the member to an intent whose `party_role` is `buyer`. `deal.Store.Agree` refuses to cite an intent naming a seller unless the agreement is the buyer's and its `counterparty_key` is `seller.key`. Two positive fixtures (`seller-name/`), six negatives and two authorization vectors cover it. A reader from an earlier release refuses an intent that carries it.

### Changed: a settlement report's receipts draw more of what the copy records (capsule-viewer#435, #444)

- `report/go.mod` and `capsulectl/go.mod` require capsule-viewer/go at bebf078. The x-deal-v0 receipts a settlement report carries (`capsulectl book report build --receipts`) now say how the copy records the payment as settled, checking a Lightning payment proof in the page when the copy carries one, and the amount's size band; who the deal was with and their words accepting the offer, only where the page recomputes them against what the copy sealed; whether each act came after the holder's yes; the sealed report's authority blocks, shown as the holder's own sealed text and not checked by the page; and the deal id, and on an adjudicator's copy the referee its report names. A seller's copy draws the settlement line too.

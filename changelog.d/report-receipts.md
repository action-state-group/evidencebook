### Added: `report.View.Receipts` carries deal receipts into a settlement report (#764)

- `report.Build` takes `View.Receipts`: the receipt module a page opens a deal's receipt with (`capsulectl.deal-view/v0`, its manifest pinning the script and stylesheets; the composed receipt module is refused until a composed bundle, rooted at the composer's composition record, can be bound to its deal) and, for every deal the Result's claims name (the part of a claim id before `::`), either that deal's receipt bundle or `Held`, the holder's share policy keeping it off the page ([capsule-viewer#228](https://github.com/action-state-group/capsule-viewer/issues/228)). The report never evaluates the policy: the caller states it, and nothing is carried by default, so a `View` with no `Receipts` builds the same page as before.
- `Build` refuses, before rendering, a deal with no entry or more than one, an entry for a deal no claim names, a bundle that does not pass the page gate (`cli.PageGate`, the verdict a deal page is written under) or that the module's manifest does not take (its `requires` and `forbids` extensions), a bundle whose root is not a capsule that deal's claims cite as evidence (the deal's exchange capsule, so a receipt filed under the wrong deal is refused), receipts on a Result with no deal, and receipts on a diagnostic page. `Page.Permalink` refuses a page with receipts: the permalink carries the bundle alone.
- `report/go.mod` requires capsule-viewer go at the commit that adds `OfflineOptions.Receipts` ([capsule-viewer#351](https://github.com/action-state-group/capsule-viewer/pull/351)), which refreshes the page runtime: the three committed report pages under `report/testdata/presentation` are rebuilt.

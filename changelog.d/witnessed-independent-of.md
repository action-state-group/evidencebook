### Changed: the `witnessed` member names the witness row's `independent_of` and `own_witness` (#1073)

- `capsulectl-deal verify --bundle F --stamp-proof P --witness-directory W` now reports, in an x-deal-v1 or x-deal-v0 copy's `witnessed` member, the `independent_of` list of the W row that names the witness, as W states it (`[]` when the row has none; left out when it is not a list of strings). It also reports `own_witness: true` when that row says `"own_witness": true`, meaning W marks this witness as its own; the member is left out otherwise. On `failed` and `withheld` the member also names that row (`witness`, `independent_of` and `own_witness`) when W lists the witness of a receipt: the first receipt that does not verify, or else the first receipt W lists. `deal report --html` carries the member to the page unchanged; the page decides from the row whether to say the witness is not listed as independent of the parties, and never says it for a witness W marks as its own (capsule-viewer#508, capsule-viewer#513). verify reads W once and checks the receipts against that same copy.

### Added: `report.View.Coverage` states the range a report over time windows covers ([#689](https://github.com/action-state-group/evidencebook/issues/689))

- A report over a holder's time windows (window books, #689) is built with `Coverage{From, To, Windows}`, the range its caller's coverage check found every window of answered. The page opens with a banner in the shell's own `aac-banner` classes: "Covers <from> to <to>. Each of the N windows has its answer from the holder's book for that window, and the windows link with no gap", followed by what the check does not show (without witness receipts the continuity is as the holder signed it; no check shows that an answer holds every row of its window). Like a wording layer, the coverage lives in the page, not in the bundle, so a page built with one gets no permalink. A coverage with no range or no window, or on a diagnostic page, is refused; a range with a gap is never passed in, since its caller builds no page for it.

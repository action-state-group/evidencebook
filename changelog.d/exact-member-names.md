### Fixed: a statement reads the same at admission, after reopen and in every language (#1072)

- **The bug.** A `subject_root` stating `{"subject_id":"b","SUBJECT_ID":"a"}` was admitted as subject `a`, because Go's JSON decoder matches member names without case and keeps the last; the book stored the canonical header, which read as `b` after reopen. A key range proof for `a` then went from three matches to none, and members under `a` were refused. A lifecycle statement spelled the same way could change a record's retention state across a reopen.
- **The fix.** A book holds every statement in its canonical form, so admission, the record `Append` returns and a reopened book read the same bytes; a statement repeating a member name, or holding invalid UTF-8, is refused. The strict readers (subject, window, book close and disclosure policy statements, disclosed headers, `index_root` statements in a subject bundle, `capsulectl-deal`'s evidence request and answer file, `capsulectl-book`'s backfill source and service file, and `report`'s presentation block) read member names exactly and once (`encoding/json/v2`), as the Rust and Python readers do. `testdata/subject/admission.json` now refuses case variants of a statement's member names.
- **Upgrading.** A book holding a `subject_root` an earlier release admitted under a case variant of a member name does not open: dropping its subject silently would also drop the close that bounds its members.

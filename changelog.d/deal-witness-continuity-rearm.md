### Fixed: one continuity 409 from the witness no longer stops a deal profile's time-stamps (evidencebook-skills#358)

- `capsulectl/go.mod` requires capsule-cli 052a8a6a (capsule-cli#648 and #649). `deal tick` re-arms a checkpoint the witness refused with a continuity 409 once, a minute or more after the refusal, and holds later checkpoints behind it, instead of storing the refusal as final. Before, every later checkpoint of that profile chained on the refused one and was refused too, so nothing more was time-stamped and a History card left out the deals agreed after it.
- Its status reads "pending: the witness refused it as not continuing the checkpoint it last accepted (HTTP 409); it is sent once more at a later capsulectl run, a minute or more after the refusal." A second refusal after that retry is re-anchored on the deal profile's own log (capsule-cli#649): capsulectl signs the same checkpoint again with the witness's last accepted checkpoint as its predecessor and sends that, so later checkpoints chain on it and get their receipts. While that is due it reads "pending: the witness refused it again after a retry, ... capsulectl proves it again from that checkpoint at a later capsulectl run, and later checkpoints wait for it."; once done, "not delivered: ... capsulectl re-anchored it ...; the receipt for the re-anchor is not kept." `deal tick` sends a checkpoint due for its re-anchor again at the next tick, oldest first and holding the later ones, even when no new checkpoint is due: the log's own pending list leaves out every row stored as failed, so before, a re-anchor that got no answer waited for the next checkpoint and the tick reported nothing pending (`capsulectl/internal/deal/deal_cadence.go`). A book window's witness store keeps no tree, so a window checkpoint refused twice is not re-anchored and reads as not delivered.
- `book window publish` shows a first continuity refusal as `pending`, as the core's own status does, including for the predecessor checkpoint `book window convert` queued, and `failed` only once it is refused again or refused for good (`capsulectl/internal/book/window_cmd.go`).

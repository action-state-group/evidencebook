### Added: the held store and `held_bundle`, a peer bundle a Close relied on, held in full (#116)

- `Book.Close` with a `PeerClose` now holds the peer bundle its tallies rely on: it appends a `held_bundle` record (`RecordTypeHeldBundle`) whose one payload is the bundle's octets as received and whose statement is `HeldBundleStatement` (`bundle_digest`, `peer`, `relied_by`), after the Close's window and before the Close, which cites it in its header. The Close body is unchanged. The peer bundle must carry its octets, which `VerifyBundle` now keeps as `VerifiedBundle.Bytes`; the tallies are computed from those octets re-read, and a bundle without them, or whose octets are another bundle, is refused. A Close retried after a failure reuses the uncited `held_bundle`. `Reconcile`, and a Close without a peer Close, hold nothing.
- `SetRetention` refuses to delete a `held_bundle` record while a kept record cites it (`ErrHeldCited`); a Close has no retention state, so the bundle a Close relied on stays.
- A bundle rooted at such a Close withholds the `held_bundle` payload by digest unless the request sets `BundleRequest.DiscloseHeld` or asks for every payload, so it discloses no more of the counterparty than before.
- `Shelf.Held(citingRecordID, digest)` reads one copy of a held item by the record that commits it, any log's, the journal's included; `Shelf.HeldBy(digest)` lists every copy (`HeldCopy`), each with its own retention state and whether its bytes remain, and the received store's copy or tombstone. The digest is SHA-256 of the octets held.

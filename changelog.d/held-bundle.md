### Added: the held store and `held_bundle`, a peer bundle a Close relied on, held in full (#116)

- `Book.Close` with a `PeerClose` now holds the peer bundle its tallies rely on: it appends a `held_bundle` record (`RecordTypeHeldBundle`) whose one payload is the bundle's octets as received and whose statement is `HeldBundleStatement` (`bundle_digest`, `peer`, `relied_by`), after the Close's window and before the Close, which cites it in its header. The Close body is unchanged. `VerifyBundle` now keeps the bundle's octets as `VerifiedBundle.Bytes`, and the tallies are computed from those octets re-read. A Close retried after a failure reuses the uncited `held_bundle`; the window ends before it, so the held bundle stays outside the window it is evidence for. `Reconcile`, and a Close without a peer Close, hold nothing.
- `SetRetention` refuses to delete a `held_bundle` record while a kept record cites it (`ErrHeldCited`), a Close not yet appended included; a Close has no retention state, so the bundle a Close relied on stays.
- On a windowed store, a window in its grace takes the `held_bundle` a citing Close appends, and only for a Close it would take: the Close is checked against the window before its bundle is held. `VerifyWindowChain` accepts such a `held_bundle` in a boundary's tail when a later close/v1 in the tail cites it; a late `held_bundle` no Close cites is a named finding.
- `Book.Acknowledge` names the counterparty's Close by its record id and keeps no copy of it: no reconcile relies on it, so the counterparty's account is held by digest.
- A bundle carries a `held_bundle` payload only when the request sets `BundleRequest.DiscloseHeld`; under payloads=selected it is otherwise withheld by digest, so a bundle discloses no more of the counterparty than before.
- `Shelf.Held(citingRecordID, digest)` reads one copy of a held item by the record that commits it, any log's, the journal's included; `Shelf.HeldBy(digest)` lists every copy (`HeldCopy`), each with its own retention state and whether its bytes remain, and the received store's copy or tombstone. The digest is SHA-256 of the octets held.

### Changed: a Close citing a peer Close, and payloads=all over a held bundle, refuse what they accepted before (#116)

- `Book.Close` with a `PeerClose` refuses (`ErrInvalid`) a peer bundle without its octets (`VerifiedBundle.Bytes`, which `VerifyBundle` sets), or whose octets are another bundle than the one it names, before anything is written. A caller that builds a `VerifiedBundle` by hand, or keeps only its parsed fields, now passes the bundle `VerifyBundle` returned.
- `Book.Bundle` with payloads=all refuses (`ErrInvalid`) a selection that holds a `held_bundle` record unless the request sets `DiscloseHeld`, since payloads=all cannot withhold its payload.

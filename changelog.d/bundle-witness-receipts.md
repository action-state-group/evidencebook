### Added: carry witness receipts in a bundle (#397)

- `BundleRequest.Witnesses` and `BundleRequest.WitnessDirectory`: the witness receipts (`checkpoint.witnesses` entries: `ts_url`, `entry_hash`, `receipt_b64`, `leaf_index`, `tree_size`) for the anchor pinned by `At`, and the directory they are checked under. `Book.Bundle` checks each receipt against the anchor's signed checkpoint before building and refuses (`ErrInvalid`, no bundle and no disclosure record) a receipt that does not verify, any receipt without a directory, and more than `MaxReplyReceipts`; it then carries them in `checkpoint.witnesses`. With this a seal of an unavailable-window statement can register without editing the bundle JSON. `BundleCheckpoint` gains `Witnesses`.

### Added: `xdealv1.SharedOperator` marks a ruling "not independent (shared operator)" from a controller binding ([#780](https://github.com/action-state-group/evidencebook/issues/780))

- `SharedOperator(ruling, agreement, bindings, revocations)` returns `NotIndependentSharedOperator` ("not independent (shared operator)") when a valid controller binding with claim `same_controller_asserted` joins the agreement's adjudicator key to the ruling's buyer or seller key, and "" otherwise. A binding is judged at the ruling's stamped time, so a revocation after the ruling does not remove the label; a bad signature, an inconclusive result or a binding of another claim shows nothing. There is no positive state: a reader shows this text or nothing, never "independent". It reads no record member and holds no list of any operator's keys. Rust and Python do not have it yet ([#782](https://github.com/action-state-group/evidencebook/issues/782)); showing it on the card is a separate change.

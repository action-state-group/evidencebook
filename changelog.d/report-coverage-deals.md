### Changed: the coverage banner names each placed deal and states the report's limit (#797)

- On a report over a deal store's windows (#797), the banner `report.Build` writes for `View.Coverage` also says that each deal placed in the windows has its answer, with each deal's line: its answer covers its whole book, records after its window included. A placed deal with no answer, or one answered at a tree the window chain does not prove, is a gap, and no page is rendered. The banner ends with the report's limit (each answer proves its rows are in the holder's log; the rows' field values are the holder's word; no proof shows that no row was left out), in place of "no check shows that an answer holds every row of its window."
- `report` now requires the evidencebook module at the commit that adds journal windows and placed deals (#846).

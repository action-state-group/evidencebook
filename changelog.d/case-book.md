### Changed: x-deal-v1 checkpoints the adjudicator's case book, one per deal (#615)

- x-deal-v1: the adjudicator's case book is one per deal and adjudicator, at a log id both parties compute (`xdealv1.CaseLogID`), and a holder refuses a ruling on any other log, so a ruling counts only from that log and a second, different ruling at one place is a fork the status reads (`conflicting_final_rulings`). A case book's stamp proof binds to the adjudicator key the agreement names, so the adjudicator checkpoints with that key; a forked case book makes no checkpoint claim. A ruling is admitted from the adjudicator whoever carries it, so a party can forward the ruling it received with its naming statement. An adjudicator holding a deal's case book admits the parties' deal-book records, to check each named its ruling. `xdealv1.HeldRuling` finds a held ruling by the record digest a statement names, and reports one not held as unavailable. PROFILE.md §4 "The case book", §5. Python does not yet refuse a ruling on another log or bind a case book proof (Rust has these rules, #774). evidencebook#615.

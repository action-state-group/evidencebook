### Added: `deal check --history`, the counterparty's verified History in the rules check (x-deal-v1, #666)

- `deal check --history FILE --counterparty-key HEX` hands the pinned rules checker, on an x-deal-v1 deal, the counterparty's `personal_history_basic/1` History as the input member `received_history`. Before anything runs or is sealed, the History is verified with capsule-cli's History verifier under `--counterparty-key`, which comes from the host's deployment policy, never from the History or anything the counterparty sent. The member names the counterparty only (`about: "counterparty"`), the keys it was verified under, and each document exactly as received, with its SHA-256 and `request_binding`.
- The check is refused, with nothing run or sealed, when a document is not accepted under the key, is not a `personal_history_basic/1` History, is a second document of its kind, or repeats an earlier one. It is also refused on an x-deal-v0 deal and without a pinned rules checker. A stapled `history_card/2` is not read yet (capsule-cli#567).
- When the checker is given the History and refuses, reports another ruleset, or answers with something unreadable, the check reports the cause alone, never the checker's own words. `--received` cards are still read only by the notice run, whose input does not hold the History.

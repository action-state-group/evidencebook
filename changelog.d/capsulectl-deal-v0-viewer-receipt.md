### Changed: an x-deal-v0 receipt page is drawn by capsule-viewer's receipt modules (#869)

- `capsulectl deal report --html` on an x-deal-v0 deal, and the receipt page a step writes, draw with capsule-viewer's x-deal-v0 receipt modules (go/receipt's `capsuleviewer.receipt.unilateral/v0` and `.seller/v0`) instead of the deal view, as the x-deal-v1 page already does. The page carries this binary's own `deal verify --bundle` report of the copy, and what only capsulectl can check reaches the page through it, in capsulectl's voice: with `--from-bundle --directory`, each countersignature as capsulectl resolved it. The page no longer warns that a step came from an older build, says today's date, or speaks a pinned wording pack's words: those lines were dropped (capsule-viewer#383). Before a page is written, capsulectl still refuses one whose words do not match their sealed commitments, or whose countersignatures do not verify or cannot be checked.
- `capsulectl deal verify --bundle` takes `--countersign-directory DIR` (with `--producer-key KEY`, repeatable): an x-deal-v0 copy's entry then reports `countersign`, each countersignature's state as `capsulectl countersign verify` reports it, bound to the copy's countersignatures by their digest. It never changes the verdict.
- The modules `capsulectl-deal` offers `capsulectl presentation build` are capsule-viewer's x-deal-v0 receipt modules and the deal view's composed module; `capsulectl.deal-view/v0` is no longer offered. `deal-view-VERSION.tar.gz` keeps its name and now holds `receipt_unilateral.js`, `receipt_seller.js`, the receipt's three stylesheets, `deal-view.js` and `deal-view.css`; unpack it again beside `capsulectl-deal` when you upgrade.
- `capsulectl/go.mod` and `report/go.mod` require capsule-viewer/go at f63283e (capsule-viewer#449, #451, #453).

- `shelf` (feature): Go's whole-shelf handover and its replay (#461 §2.7), byte for byte. `Cadence::handover` hands the shelf log over up to a finished stamp (the newest when `n` is 0) with its entry documents, status openings, book checkpoints and consistency proofs, and `verify_handover` replays it under the holder's checkpoint key and a witness directory: it refuses an entry held back or changed, a document left out or not its canonical JSON, a changed or missing opening, a book checkpoint left out or not the holder's, and a replaced book, and returns the coverage start, whether a receipt verifies, each book's latest fact and the untied books. `shelf_facts::BookFact` is added, and `shelf_facts::Opening` gains serde (`{salt, value}`).

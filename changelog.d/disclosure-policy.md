- The disclosure policy (#156): `DisclosurePolicy` is the `policy` member of loop's `disclosure-policy/0` file, shipped as "Action State default v1" for a person (Muse) or a node (Mesh), with the settings window, standard history, staple, more-than-standard, receipt, unsigned-ask rate limit, under-contract scope and asked-of-you log retention, and the never-list as fixed text. `Book.AdoptPolicy` and `Book.ApplyPolicyChange` seal it: a `policy-change/v1` block (base policy digest, round, changed fields, `then[]`) becomes one `disclosure_policy` record per field, a change composed against another round is refused `ErrStaleRound`, and one that names a never-list item is refused `ErrNeverList`. A policy record is never served. `DisclosurePolicy.Rows` is the settings page, and `Doctor` reports the same rows (`setting_*`) for a book that holds a policy, answers under it, and takes `DoctorOptions.As` (`stranger` or `signed`). `SharePolicy.StandardHistory` switches `standard_history/1` off (`derivation_unsupported`). The probe's standard-card line now states the card's window instead of the deal total.
